# Databricks Exam Coupon Notes

## Current Reality

The Databricks Certified Data Engineer Associate exam currently lists a `USD 200` registration fee on the official Databricks certification page.

The best official low-cost route is usually the Databricks Learning Festival voucher path, which has offered:

- 50% off any Databricks certification exam.
- 20% off a yearly Databricks Academy Labs subscription.

## July 2026 Status

The most recent event found was:

```text
Advanced Learning Festival: June 15 - July 6, 2026
```

The official community event says learners needed to finish all modules in at least one listed pathway during the event window to receive a 50% certification discount and 20% Academy Labs discount.

The FAQ says the 50% voucher is distributed in July 2026, sent to the email attached to the Databricks Customer Academy account, and is valid for about 90 days, expiring before October 6, 2026.

If the eligible pathway was not completed during June 15 - July 6, 2026, the likely next path is to watch for the next quarterly Learning Festival, historically around October.

## 100% Free Voucher Reality

A Databricks employee response on the 2026 Learning Festival page said none of the planned Virtual Learning Festival events would include a 100% discount voucher.

There may be special partner or employer programs, but those are not generally available to normal Customer Academy learners.

## Action Plan

1. Log in to Databricks Customer Academy.
2. Check `My Activities` and confirm whether any eligible Learning Festival pathway was completed during the June 15 - July 6, 2026 window.
3. Search email for:

```text
Databricks voucher
Learning Festival
certification discount
Customer Academy
```

4. If eligible and no voucher arrived, raise a Databricks Training Help ticket.
5. Subscribe to Databricks Community Learning Events for the next festival.
6. Avoid buying shared or third-party voucher codes unless Databricks explicitly allows transfer. Treat unofficial voucher resale as risky.
