# Return and Refund Policy

## Document Metadata

Document Owner: Customer Operations  
Document Type: Enterprise Policy  
Version: 1.0  
Status: Active  
Last Reviewed: October 2026  

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the conditions under which customers may return purchased products and receive refunds.

The policy is intended to provide a consistent process for handling product returns while reducing unnecessary operational cost and customer confusion.

---

## 2. Standard Return Window

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days of the delivery date.

The return window begins on the date the order is marked as delivered.

Return requests submitted after 30 calendar days are not automatically eligible for approval and must be reviewed under the exception process.

---

## 3. Product Condition Requirements

To qualify for a standard return, the product should normally be:

- in resalable condition,
- returned with original packaging where applicable,
- accompanied by the original order information,
- free from intentional damage or misuse.

Products that have been used beyond normal inspection may be denied a refund.

---

## 4. Damaged or Defective Products

Products reported as damaged or defective are handled separately from standard returns.

Customers must report damaged or defective merchandise within 14 calendar days of delivery.

When a damaged or defective item is confirmed, the customer may be offered one of the following:

- full refund,
- replacement product,
- store credit.

Return shipping costs for confirmed damaged or defective products are covered by the company.

---

## 5. Incorrect Items

If the customer receives an incorrect product, the issue should be treated as a fulfillment error.

Customers should report incorrect items within 14 calendar days of delivery.

The company will provide return shipping at no cost to the customer and may provide either:

- the correct replacement product, or
- a full refund.

---

## 6. Non-Returnable Items

The following product types are generally not eligible for standard returns:

- personalized or customized products,
- final-sale items,
- products damaged through misuse,
- products marked as non-returnable at the time of purchase.

Exceptions may be made if the product was damaged, defective, or shipped incorrectly.

---

## 7. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method whenever possible.

Refund processing begins after the returned product is received and validated.

Customers should generally allow 5 to 7 business days for refund processing after the return has been approved.

Bank or payment-provider processing time may add additional delay.

---

## 8. Return Shipping

For standard discretionary returns, the customer may be responsible for return shipping costs.

The company covers return shipping when the return results from:

- product damage,
- manufacturing defect,
- incorrect item shipment,
- documented fulfillment error.

---

## 9. Return Rate Monitoring

Return behavior is monitored at the product, category, and customer-segment level.

A product category should be flagged for operational review when its monthly return rate exceeds 12%.

A category that exceeds a 15% monthly return rate should receive priority review by the Product and Customer Operations teams.

These thresholds are internal operational triggers and do not represent customer eligibility rules.

---

## 10. Escalation Process

A return case should be escalated when:

- the customer disputes a denied return,
- the product value exceeds $500,
- repeated defects are reported for the same product,
- a product category exceeds internal return-rate thresholds,
- suspected fraud or abuse is identified.

Escalated cases should be reviewed by Customer Operations or the appropriate business owner.

---

## 11. Policy Exceptions

Exceptions may be granted when there is clear evidence that strict application of the standard policy would create an unreasonable customer outcome.

Exceptions should be documented with:

- reason for the exception,
- approving employee or manager,
- date of approval,
- related order identifier.

---

## 12. Important Interpretation Notes

The 30-day standard return window and the 14-day damaged-product reporting window are different rules.

A damaged product reported after 14 days may still fall within the 30-day standard return window, but it may no longer qualify for damaged-product handling.

Operational return-rate thresholds are used for business monitoring and should not be interpreted as customer-specific eligibility rules.
